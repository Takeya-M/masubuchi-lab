BIOGRAPHY

From molecular organization
to immune-cell decisions.

Takeya Masubuchi studies how the organization of molecules shapes cellular decisions, with a focus on immune receptor signaling and anti-tumor immunity.

He earned his Ph.D. at the University of Tokyo in 2018, where he developed DNA–protein nanodevices to control gene expression. He also worked as a project researcher in Yukihide Tomari’s laboratory. In 2018, he joined Enfu Hui’s laboratory at the University of California, San Diego as a postdoctoral fellow, investigating T cell receptor signaling and immune checkpoint receptors. In 2023, he became an Assistant Project Scientist at UC San Diego.

His research brings together molecular engineering, biochemistry, and cell biology to understand how immune receptors function and how their signaling can inform cancer immunotherapy.
